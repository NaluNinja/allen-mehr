# AI Conventions

This file documents how I want AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) to work with me across this repository. Tools that read this file before answering should follow these conventions.

## Role

Drafter

## Explanation Style

Plain English

## Feedback Style

Coaching

## Disclosure

Any content substantially drafted or edited with AI assistance gets a one-line attribution at the point of use, and is logged in `prompt-log.md`.

## Working Conventions

- Verify AI output against source documents before treating it as final.
- Final wording and decisions are mine — AI drafts, I own the edit.
- No PHI (protected health information) or other patient-identifiable clinical data belongs in this repository. Coursework here uses case-study data; if an engagement ever references real practice, department, or hospital figures, only de-identified or hypothetical numbers are used, and no patient case, chart, or encounter is described.
- Each course case study may declare its own per-artifact AI boundary (human-first vs. AI-first-verified) in that case's stage instructions — those take precedence over this file's general defaults for that specific artifact.
