# Bio

_Drafted with AI assistance (Claude); reviewed and finalized by Allen Mehr — see AGENTS.md and prompt-log.md._

Allen Mehr, DO is a board-certified urogynecologist and reconstructive pelvic surgeon practicing at the Spark M. Matsunaga VA Medical Center in Honolulu, Hawaii. He holds an academic appointment as Assistant Professor of Obstetrics & Gynecology at the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences and brings over a decade of clinical, academic, and departmental leadership experience, including service as Medical Director of Urogynecology & Pelvic Rehabilitation at The Women's Hospital in Evansville, Indiana, and as Deputy Chief and Chief of Gynecologic Services within the Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology at Tripler Army Medical Center.

A veteran of the U.S. Army Medical Corps (2007–2026). His research and publications focus on pelvic organ prolapse surgery outcomes and the psychosocial dimensions of overactive bladder. He is currently expanding his financial and analytical skills to support practice and department-level leadership decisions, and this repository documents that ongoing coursework.
