# Economic Research

Applying the course's micro and macro concepts to a global challenge and turning the analysis into a
defended policy recommendation. The spec (`spec.md`) names the data sources, the model, the planned
figures, and the success criteria, written before the research started.

**Exercised in:** generic drug shortages, narrowed to cisplatin and carboplatin — why cheap sterile
injectables keep going short when the market is supposed to refill them. The paper defends the Senate
Finance reliability premium with an amendment: Medicare pays the add-on only when the named alternate
manufacturer is verified and performing. The analysis is a breakeven on ASPE's cost of entry, asking
how much revenue uplift a sterile-injectable generic needs before entering pays. Brief, spec, dated
drafts, and both figures are done; the final PDF goes to `analysis/research-paper.pdf`.
