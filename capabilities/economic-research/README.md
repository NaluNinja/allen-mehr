# Economic Research

Applying the course's micro and macro concepts to a global challenge and turning the analysis into a
defended policy recommendation. The spec (`spec.md`) will name the data sources, the model, the
planned figures, and the success criteria before the research starts.

**Exercised in:** generic drug shortages — why cheap sterile injectables like cisplatin, carboplatin,
and IV saline keep going short when the market is supposed to refill them. Single-manufacturer market
structure, price-inelastic hospital demand, and procurement that rewards the lowest bid rather than
the most reliable one, against a policy fight over paying a reliability premium. The brief is written;
the spec, drafts, and paper are still to come.
